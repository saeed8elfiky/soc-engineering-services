04 / Engagement Model

Four-Phase Delivery

Clear scope, cost, and expectations from day one.

01

Discovery & Implementation Plan

Sizing

A formal implementation plan is produced before any implementation work begins — it is a deliverable in its own right.

  • Assessment of log sources, expected daily ingest volume (GB/day), and retention requirements
  • Infrastructure sizing: node count, CPU/RAM/storage, and cluster topology
  • Architecture diagram covering data flow from source to detection to alerting
  • Project timeline, milestones, and defined success criteria
02

Implementation

Build
  • Deployment and configuration of the SIEM platform per the agreed architecture
  • Log source onboarding and pipeline configuration
  • Initial detection rule set deployment and SOAR workflow build-out
03

Tuning & Handover

Go-Live
  • Alert tuning against live traffic to bring false-positive rates down to operational level
  • Knowledge transfer and documentation handover to the internal team
  • Formal go-live sign-off against success criteria set in Phase 1
04

Post-Implementation Support

6 Months Included

Every implementation includes six months of post-launch support at no additional cost.

  • Ongoing rule and workflow tuning as real-world alert patterns emerge
  • Troubleshooting of platform, ingestion, or integration issues
  • Ad-hoc incident support and investigation assistance
  • Minor scope adjustments as the environment evolves
